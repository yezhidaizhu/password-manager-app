Design a complete, original, production-quality Android app UI for "KeyNest", a privacy-first local password manager.

The interface language is Simplified Chinese. Emerald green is the brand color. The design should be bold, distinctive, contemporary, spatial, and clearly non-flat. Avoid classic or generic mobile-app design. Remove anything unnecessary.

The product stores account credentials locally, unlocks with a master password or biometrics, supports finding and managing credentials, generates passwords, and exposes essential local security settings.

You are the product designer. Decide the information architecture, screen selection, navigation, interaction model, layout, hierarchy, component forms, use of depth, materials, typography, logo treatment, and presentation yourself. Do not follow or preserve any previous design, conventional screen template, or predetermined layout. Prioritize a coherent real product over visual gimmicks.

Present the resulting key app interfaces together as one polished design board. This must be a shippable app interface, not a landing page, mood board, wireframe, or marketing presentation. No watermark.
