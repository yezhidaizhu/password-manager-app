Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: bold high-fidelity Android password-manager concept board
Primary request: Completely redesign the local password manager "KeyNest" using a fearless high-contrast pink visual system inspired by contemporary Chinese digital culture. Show exactly four essential app screens: unlock, vault list, account detail, and edit with password generator. Remove every nonessential decoration and secondary feature.

Brand idea: a minimal geometric cat-head symbol with a keyhole cutout. Make it iconic, sharp, and memorable, never childish or mascot-like.

Visual direction: neo-editorial product UI with a slight poster-design influence, strong black typography, surprising off-center alignment, flat blocks, sharp dividers, large numerals, small technical labels, and controlled visual tension. It should feel energetic and original while remaining usable as a real security tool. Avoid standard Material cards and conventional settings-app styling. Mostly square or 4px corners, graphic circular controls, no gradients and no glass.

Screens:
1. Unlock: bold pink field occupying a meaningful portion of the screen, cat-keyhole mark, "KeyNest", one master-password field, one black or pink "解锁" action, compact fingerprint control, tiny text "仅存储在此设备". Nothing else.
2. Vault list: strong "保险库" header, minimal search, borderless list of GitHub, Gmail, AWS and Notion credentials, username secondary text, restrained favorites, one graphic add control. No filter chips unless essential.
3. Account detail: "GitHub" as the main typographic anchor; username, masked password, website, notes; copy and reveal actions integrated with values; compact "编辑" action; destructive delete placed far away. No cards around individual fields.
4. Edit plus generator: fields for "名称", "网址 / App", "用户名", "密码", "备注"; integrated generator panel with length 20, graphic slider, three minimal toggles, generated password, and "使用此密码". Keep the structure sparse.

Composition/framing: 2048x1152 landscape concept board, exactly four straight-on portrait app canvases in one row, equal scale, fully visible, no realistic phone hardware, no perspective, no overlap. Small neutral captions beneath each canvas. Preserve readable Chinese text.
Color palette: signature vivid pink #FB7299 as the primary, ink black #151015, warm white #FFF8FA, soft pink surface #FFE8EF, muted gray #766A70, thin border #E6C8D2, electric cyan #20C9C3 used only as a rare information accent, warning #E39A16, danger #D92D4F. Pink can occupy large flat fields; do not turn the whole UI into pale pink.
Typography: modern Simplified Chinese grotesk sans, extreme but controlled weight contrast, large functional headings, compact technical labels, zero letter spacing.
Text requirement: render quoted Chinese and English text verbatim and legibly; no invented labels.
Constraints: unconventional and design-forward; not cute; no classic Material look; no generic rounded cards; no nested cards; no bottom navigation; no gradients; no glassmorphism; no decorative shadows; no blue or purple dominance; no beige; no emoji; no stock imagery; no 3D; no watermark; no browser UI; no explanatory feature copy.
